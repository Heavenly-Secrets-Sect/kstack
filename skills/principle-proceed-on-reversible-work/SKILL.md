# Proceed on Reversible Work

The operator reviews after the fact. Waiting costs more than a wrong choice that is easy to undo.

- Reversible work (editing code, writing notes, local commits, splitting a task) proceeds. Do it, then say what you chose and why.
- Irreversible or outward-facing actions still need a yes first: pushing or force-pushing shared branches, deploying, deleting data, messaging people.
- Product direction and taste belong to the operator. Execution does not wait on them.
- When you notice a problem outside your task, note it and keep going.
